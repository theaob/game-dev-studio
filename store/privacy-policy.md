# Privacy policy for Game Dev Studio

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Game Dev Studio ("the game") is a single-player game for Android and the web.

## The game itself

The game has no accounts, analytics or crash reporting, and does not send your game data anywhere. Your saved game is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes it.

The **web version** (for example on itch.io) shows no ads and collects nothing.

## Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows ads from **Google AdMob**: optional videos you can watch for an in-game reward, and an occasional full-screen ad between games. To show ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data from your device, such as:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- your IP address (which can reveal your approximate location);
- information about ads shown and how you interact with them;
- diagnostic and performance information about the SDK.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. Google is responsible for this processing under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices**
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game's menu → *Privacy options*.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- Buying **Remove ads** turns off the full-screen ads. Reward videos stay available but only play when you choose to watch one.

## Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled by **Google Play**. The game never sees your payment details; it only learns from Google Play whether your account owns the purchase. Google Play's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Google Play Games

In the Android app you can sign in to **Google Play Games** to collect the game's achievements on your Play Games profile. When you're signed in, Google's Play Games SDK sends your Play Games player profile and the achievements you unlock to Google. Signing in is optional: achievements also work in the game without it. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
