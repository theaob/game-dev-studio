# Privacy policy for Game Dev Studio

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Game Dev Studio ("the game") is a single-player game for Android and the web.

## What the game collects

**Nothing.** The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or usage data.

- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
- The game makes no network requests while you play. It works offline.
- Your saved game is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes it.

## Permissions

The Android app does not ask for any permissions that require your approval. It includes the standard internet permission that every app built with its framework (Capacitor) has, but the game does not use it.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
